I read the page. You wrote four subsequences — $[2]$, $[2,4]$, $[4,6]$, $[2,4,6]$ — braced them together and concluded all of them have GCD 2.

Every one of those four does have GCD 2. That is not the problem.

The problem is that $[2,4,6]$ has **seven** non-empty strictly increasing subsequences, and you listed four. You generalised off a partial enumeration, and the entries you did not write down are precisely the ones the question was about.

So the answer to *what are you saying* is: write the other three down and you will see it. I am not going to say which they are.

## Your move

Then tell me the two counts I actually asked for: how many of all seven have every element divisible by 2, and how many have GCD exactly 2.
