- [ ] 5-FOLD CV OVER THE WHOLE PIPELINE. Added 2026-08-28. NOT BUILT.
      Every number in the package rests on ONE 80/20 partition. Two
      consequences: the estimand is the fifth of the cohort that landed in
      test, and nothing separates a real effect from a lucky partition.
      SHAPE: wrap the existing per-contrast chain in a 5-fold loop so every
      eligible patient serves in exactly one test fold and the union of folds
      is the WHOLE eligible population, not 20% of it. Folds STRATIFIED ON
      INDEX ARM AND TRD LABEL JOINTLY -- the estimator's behaviour depends on
      both the arm balance and the event rate, and the current split happens
      to be balanced on both (see the table above); the folds must reproduce
      that by design rather than inherit it by luck.
      COST IS NOT THE OBSTACLE: three contrasts currently run in 4m20s total,
      so 5x is ~22 minutes, well inside the existing 4h wall. This is a
      design decision, not a compute one.
      TWO QUESTIONS TO ANSWER FIRST:
      (1) Does CV REPLACE the shared frozen split here, or sit beside it? The
          frozen split is what makes this package's gradeable metrics
          comparable to Paper 1's classical-ML numbers. Replacing it breaks
          that comparison; adding it means reporting everything twice. Likely
          answer: shared split stays the headline for the GRADEABLE half, CV
          becomes the robustness layer for the EFFECT estimates. Decide it.
      (2) How do fold results combine? Averaging point estimates across folds
          is easy. The interval is not: pooling all fold-by-draw averages into
          one percentile cut CONFLATES two different sources of variation,
          since between-fold spread measures sensitivity to the partition
          while the bootstrap measures sampling and estimation uncertainty.
          RECOMMENDED: report three separate quantities -- fold-averaged point
          estimate, bootstrap interval, between-fold spread. Do not fuse them.
      KNOCK-ON: band membership is recomputed per fold as well as per draw, so
      the trimmed share becomes a per-fold quantity and belongs in the
      population report above, not only in effect_results.json.
      KNOCK-ON 2: the bootstrap already refits three models per draw; folding
      multiplies that by five. The joblib chunking in bootstrap_effect assumes
      one population per call, so the fold loop goes OUTSIDE it -- do not try
      to parallelise folds and draws in the same Parallel call.
