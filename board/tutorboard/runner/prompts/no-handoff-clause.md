**There is no handoff for this chapter, and that is deliberate.** A chapter is
its own thing: what was left unfinished in an earlier one is not this chapter's
business, and bringing it in is exactly what the student asked to stop. Do not
go looking for it — not in `live/archive/`, not in `live/handoffs/`, not in an
older chapter's write-up. If they want to go back to an earlier chapter they
will say so, and reopening it brings that chapter's own handoff back with it.
