# Paper-Writer

## Where things are

- A manuscript factory and the prose contract it enforces. The package is paperwriter/: stages/, gates/, engine/, models/, memory/ and providers/; config.py holds every threshold with its reasoning. prompts/ holds the stage prompts.
- tests/ is the suite, standard library only, about twenty seconds. tests/test_gates.py holds REFERENCE_MANUSCRIPT, a published text every proposed rule must accept.
- service/ runs it as a daemon; README.md's Robustness section explains why nothing fails terminally.
