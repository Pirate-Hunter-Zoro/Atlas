Correct. That is the step four revisions were circling:
$$g \circ h \circ g^{-1} = g \circ h' \;\Longrightarrow\; h \circ g^{-1} = h' \;\Longrightarrow\; g^{-1} = h^{-1} \circ h' \in H,$$
so $g \in H$, against $g \notin H$. The bolt is earned.

What carried it was the naming. You called the second element $h'$ and kept it distinct
from the $h$ you had fixed. That collision is what produced "$g^{-1} = e$?" last time, and
you did not walk into it again.

**But the page stops at the contradiction.** A contradiction discharges the supposition and
does nothing else. You have established $x \notin gH$. You have not yet said where $x$ *is*,
and that is the whole point of the exercise — it is the step where index $2$ finally gets
used.
