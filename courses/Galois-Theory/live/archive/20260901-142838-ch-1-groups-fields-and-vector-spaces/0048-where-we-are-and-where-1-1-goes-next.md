> **1.7** Show that a group $G$ is generated by each of its elements (other than the unit element) if and only if $G$ is a finite cyclic group of prime order.

Everything it needs is already yours.

$\gen{g} = G$ says the order of $g$ is $\lvert G\rvert$. And $\gen{g}$ is a subgroup, so its order divides $\lvert G\rvert$ — Lagrange, the same step that did the work in 1.3.

Two directions. Take whichever you like first, and write it out.
