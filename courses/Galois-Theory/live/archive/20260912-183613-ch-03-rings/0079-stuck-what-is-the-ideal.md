The step you are missing is the one about $(a)$, and it is easier to see in a
ring where units are plentiful, so go there first.

$\mathbb{Q}$ is a field, so every non-zero element is a unit. Take $a = 2$, and
recall
$$(2) = \{2r : r \in \mathbb{Q}\}.$$

$(2)$ looks at first like it should be "the even ones". It is not — in
$\mathbb{Q}$ you may divide.
