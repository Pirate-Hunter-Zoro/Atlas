title: Negation is the additive inverse, nothing more. Multiplication is closed.
---

**Your question first.** *Negation* here is not logical negation and not
subtraction. It is the additive inverse: the negation of $x$ is the element
$-x$ with $x + (-x) = 0$. "Closed under negation" says that whenever $x$ lies
in $R$, the element $-x$ lies in $R$ too.

Why a subring wants it: a subring has to be a ring in its own right, so it has
to be a group under addition, and a group must contain the inverse of each of
its elements. Closure under addition and negation together are exactly what
makes $R$ an additive subgroup of $\mathbb{Q}$. Without negation you have a
set closed under $+$ and $\times$ but with no subtraction in it.

So the condition to check is one line, and it is the same kind of line as the
two you have already written: take $x = a/b$ in $R$, name a representation of
$-x$, and say what its denominator is. The arithmetic fact you need is
$-(a/b) = (-a)/b$ — the sign moves to the numerator and the denominator is
untouched. Membership in $R$ never looks at the numerator.

**Multiplication.** Correct, and you were right that it is the addition
argument again: $(a/b)(c/d) = ac/(bd)$, denominator $bd$, and $p \nmid bd$ by
the contrapositive of Euclid's lemma, which you stated correctly a second
time. That is now twice that primality has done the same job in the same
place.

One spelling, since you will write the word in every remaining part of this
exercise: **principal** ideal domain, not *principle*. A principal ideal is
one generated by a single element.

Two conditions of the four are still unwritten: $1 \in R$, and negation.

**Exercise 3.17, part one.** With $p$ a fixed prime, show that

$$R = \left\{ x \in \mathbb{Q} : x = r/s \text{ for some } r,s \in \mathbb{Z} \text{ with } s \neq 0 \text{ and } p \nmid s \right\}$$

is a **subring** of $\mathbb{Q}$.

**Recall.**

- *Subring:* a subset $S \subseteq \mathbb{Q}$ that contains $1$, is closed
  under addition, is closed under negation, and is closed under
  multiplication.
- *Closed under negation:* $x \in S \Rightarrow -x \in S$, where $-x$ is the
  additive inverse of $x$.
- *Membership in $R$:* $x \in R$ iff **at least one** representation $r/s$ of
  $x$ has $p \nmid s$.
- *$n \mid m$:* $m = nk$ for some integer $k$. *$n \nmid m$:* no such $k$.
- *$p$ prime:* $p > 1$, and its only positive divisors are $1$ and $p$.
- *Euclid's lemma:* $p$ prime and $p \mid bd$ $\Rightarrow$ $p \mid b$ or
  $p \mid d$. Contrapositive: $p \nmid b$ and $p \nmid d$ $\Rightarrow$
  $p \nmid bd$.
- *Arithmetic in $\mathbb{Q}$:* $a/b + c/d = (ad+bc)/(bd)$;
  $-(a/b) = (-a)/b$; $(a/b)(c/d) = ac/(bd)$.
- *Done already:* closure under addition, and closure under multiplication,
  both on your page.
