## 3.12(a)

**Show that the units of $R = \mathbb{Z} + i\sqrt{5}\,\mathbb{Z}$ are $1$ and
$-1$.**

**What the statement uses.**

- *Unit:* $\alpha \in R$ such that $\alpha\beta = 1$ for some $\beta \in R$.
- $\varphi(m + i\sqrt{5}\,n) = m^{2} + 5n^{2}$, a non-negative integer.
- $\varphi(0) = 0$, $\varphi(1) = \varphi(-1) = 1$, and $\varphi(\alpha) > 3$
  for every other $\alpha \in R$.
- $\varphi(\alpha\beta) = \varphi(\alpha)\varphi(\beta)$.
