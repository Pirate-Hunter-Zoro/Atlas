title: The skeleton is the right proof. The last line is false, and one word is missing from all of it.

The idea is correct and it is the standard argument: finiteness forces the
powers of $x$ to repeat, repetition hands you a power of $x$ equal to $e$, and
the power one below that is the inverse.

Then the last line says **all** elements have an inverse.

They do not. $0$ has no inverse in a field — $e \neq 0$ is part of the
definition, and $0 \cdot b = 0$ for every $b$. What the exercise wants is that
every element of $R^{*}$ is a unit.

And that is not a slip of wording. The word *non-zero* is absent from your whole
page, and it is absent at exactly the place where the work happens: nothing you
have written says why
$$x^{n} = x \quad\text{lets you conclude}\quad x^{n-1} = e.$$
That line is doing the entire proof and it arrives with no reason attached.
