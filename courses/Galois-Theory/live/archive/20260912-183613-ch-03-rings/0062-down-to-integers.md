Your constraint is exactly the right one: the product must be $1$ and the
coefficients must stay in $\mathbb{Z}$. Nothing wrong with that.

But that constraint does not say what you think it says, and the place to see
it is one level below polynomials — in $\mathbb{Z}$ itself, with no $x$ in
sight. You are picturing "an inverse" as a reciprocal $1/a$ and then noticing
$1/a$ is not an integer. True for most $a$. Not for all of them.

---

**Your move.** Forget $R[x]$. In $\mathbb{Z}$ alone:

List **every** pair of integers $(a, b)$ with
$$ab = 1.$$

Both entries must be integers. There is more than one pair.
