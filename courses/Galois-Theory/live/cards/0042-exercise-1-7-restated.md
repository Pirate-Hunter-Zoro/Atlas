You asked for the statement, so here it is, out of the book:

> **1.7** Show that a group $G$ is generated by each of its elements (other than the unit element) if and only if $G$ is a finite cyclic group of prime order.

That is Garling's 1.7. It is about cyclic groups of prime order and it has nothing to do with $S_4$.

**So the record needs correcting, and the fault is mine.** The "Exercise 1.7" you have been looking at for the last few cards — *find the order-$4$ subgroups of $S_4$ and say which are normal* — is not in the book. It was invented and then labelled 1.7. I repeated the label on card 0038 and again a moment ago. Wrong both times.

The mathematics in it was real and you did the good half of it yourself: $\gen{(1234)}$ holds two of the six $4$-cycles, so conjugation walks out and it is not normal. Keep that. It was just never exercise 1.7.

While correcting: card 0041 said the rest of §1.1 was "fields, polynomials, $[L:K]$, irreducibility". Also wrong. §1.1 is groups all the way through — 1.1 inverses, 1.2 the first isomorphism theorem, 1.4 a unique subgroup of order $k$ is normal, 1.6 every permutation is a product of transpositions, 1.8 generators of $C_n$, 1.9 a non-abelian group of order $8$ with every subgroup normal, 1.10 the sign homomorphism and $A_n$. Fields start at 1.11.

You said you would decide. So: **do you want 1.7 as the book actually states it?**
