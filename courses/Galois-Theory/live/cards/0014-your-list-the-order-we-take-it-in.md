Your third one, **$H$ of index 2 is normal**, is exercise 1.3. It is done, transcribed and
compiled. Struck off.

The four we take, in this order:

1. **$H$ = all elements of order dividing $n$ is normal.** It runs on one fact — conjugation does
   not change order — and that fact is the engine of the next one too.
2. **$H$ the *only* subgroup of order $n$ is normal.** Same engine, aimed at the whole subgroup
   instead of one element. This is the cleanest normality argument there is.
3. **$|G|$ even $\Rightarrow$ some $g \neq e$ with $g^2 = e$.** A counting argument, not a
   conjugation one — your own margin note, *something is its own inverse*, is exactly the idea.
4. **1.7** — $G$ generated by each of its non-identity elements iff $G$ is finite of prime order.
   Lagrange and element order, together.

1.6, 1.9, 1.13, 1.14, 1.18 and the ideal material wait; 1.9 in particular wants the quaternions
built first.
