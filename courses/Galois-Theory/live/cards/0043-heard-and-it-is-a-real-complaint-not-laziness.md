Death by example is exactly what it was. Two subgroups, four elements each, conjugate things by other things and read off the survivors. There is no idea in it. The cycle-type shortcut saves you the arithmetic; it does not make the question interesting, because the question was not interesting.

And it was not an exercise. I have now checked the book: *find the order-$4$ subgroups of $S_4$ and say which are normal* **is not in Garling**. An earlier card invented it and stamped "1.7" on it, and I repeated the label twice without looking. You have been grinding on something nobody assigned.

Here is what 1.7 actually says:

> Show that a group $G$ is generated by each of its elements other than the unit element if and only if $G$ is a finite cyclic group of prime order.

No enumeration. No cases. Two directions, a few lines each, and both of them turn on Lagrange — the thing you already used in 1.3 without being told to. It is the opposite kind of problem to the one you just threw out, and it is the sort the rest of this book is made of.

Card 0042 has the full corrected picture of what §1.1 contains, if you want to see what you are choosing between.

**Do you want 1.7, or have you had enough of §1.1 for tonight?**
