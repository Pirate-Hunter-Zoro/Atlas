One correction to the margin, though. A set of conjugates is **not** obliged to be a subgroup, so "this is not a subgroup" is not by itself evidence of an error — the three correct conjugates will not form one either. You were right that something was broken, but not for that reason. The check that would have caught it is a different one, and you will see it as soon as the three are written down.
