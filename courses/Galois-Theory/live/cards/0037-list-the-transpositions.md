You have the right call: $\langle (1234)\rangle$ is not normal in $S_4$, and $V_4$ is the one to bet on. And you have spotted the obstruction — conjugation sends a $4$-cycle to a $4$-cycle, and the subgroup $\langle(1234)\rangle$ is not all the $4$-cycles in $S_4$, so it cannot be a union of conjugacy classes.

Before we close the question, one mechanical check.

A **transposition** in $S_4$ is a $2$-cycle: $(12), (13), (14), (23), (24), (34)$ — six of them. Write the full list, one per line. Then tick each off if (and only if) it appears in $V_4$ or in $\langle(1234)\rangle$.
