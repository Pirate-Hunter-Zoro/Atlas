## 3.12(b)

possible in $\mathbb{N}$ — except the number on the right is no longer $1$.

**What the statement uses.**

- *Irreducible:* $\alpha \in R$ is irreducible if $\alpha \neq 0$, $\alpha$ is
  not a unit, and whenever $\alpha = \beta\gamma$ with $\beta, \gamma \in R$,
  one of $\beta$, $\gamma$ is a unit.
- $\varphi(m + i\sqrt{5}\,n) = m^{2} + 5n^{2}$, and
  $\varphi(\alpha\beta) = \varphi(\alpha)\varphi(\beta)$.
- $\varphi(0) = 0$, $\varphi(1) = \varphi(-1) = 1$, and $\varphi(\alpha) > 3$
  for every other $\alpha \in R$.
- The units of $R$ are $1$ and $-1$ — yours, from (a).
