**What the statement uses.**

- $R$ is a commutative ring with identity $1$.
- $(a) = \{ra : r \in R\}$, the ideal generated by $a$.
- $a$ is a **unit**: there exists $a^{-1} \in R$ with $a^{-1}a = 1$.
