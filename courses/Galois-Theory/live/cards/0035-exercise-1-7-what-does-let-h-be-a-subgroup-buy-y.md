title: Order-4 subgroups of S4 — which one is normal?
---
*This card was originally labelled "Exercise 1.7". That label was wrong — this question is not in Garling. See card 0042.*

Take $G = S_4$, and let $H$ be **any** subgroup of order $4$.

**Claim.** There are exactly two subgroups of order $4$ in $S_4$ up to conjugation: the Klein four-group $V_4 = \{e, (12)(34), (13)(24), (14)(23)\}$, and the cyclic group $\gen{(1234)}$.

Before you prove anything: which of these two does *not* contain any 2-cycles? Which one is *not* normal in $S_4$?

Tell me, in one sentence each, which is which — and which one you would bet is the normal one.
