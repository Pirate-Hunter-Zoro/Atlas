# Plan

Garling's Chapter 4 exercises come first. The worksheet follows them.

## Book exercises, Chapter 4

Open, in teaching order: **4.8, 4.10, 4.7**, then **4.11**, then **4.3**. 4.8 is
the general fact behind 4.9, 4.10 uses 4.8, 4.7 is a counting argument. 4.3 is
the hardest in the chapter, so it goes last.

**4.2 is out.** It was skipped and it stays skipped.

Closed, and never re-taught: **4.1, 4.4, 4.5, 4.6, 4.9**.

## Then the worksheet

"Field Extensions and the Ring $F[x]$", six problems, sent as photographs and
read. Order: **6**, then **3(a)** and **3(c)**, then **1**, **5**, **2**, **4**.
Part 3(b) is exercise 4.6, already proved, so it is not re-posed. Problem 6 is
half-open — the student was asked to square $\gamma = \sqrt2 + \sqrt3$ and never
answered, and it is re-posed from the top when the book is clear.

## Write-up
