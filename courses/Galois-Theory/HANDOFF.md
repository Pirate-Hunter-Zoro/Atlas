What was wrong. The proof shape, not the algebra. They assumed L = K
needs a contradiction, supposed some l3 not in K, derived l1 = k^{-1}*l3
and stalled: "I'm having trouble reaching the contradiction." Two things
under it. They fed the spanning property an element they knew nothing
about instead of the one element of L known to lie in K, namely 1. And
they had not registered that K is contained in L by hypothesis, so only
the reverse inclusion needs proving. Card 0003 says both and is open.
