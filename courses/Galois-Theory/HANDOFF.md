## How this student works

They skip, and skipping is them setting the pace — three skips in ten minutes at one point. A skip
means *I have this*: move on, do not re-ask, do not remark on it.

**They will tell you when a question is worthless, bluntly.** At 14:13: "This is awful. Not a clever
proof. Just death by example. Fuck this question." They were right — it was case-checking with no
idea in it. (That is *not* the override phrase; do not treat it as one.) Choose exercises that turn
on one structural fact over ones that enumerate. This is the single most useful thing known about
teaching them.

"Not understanding" means *the step before did not land* — go back a whole step, usually to
notation. Never introduce a symbol inside the question that uses it. They never name a group they
have not been shown. They patch the exact character you name and nothing adjacent — repeat a
request in a *new* form. **Read the margins**: their corrections arrive as marginal annotations with
arrows, and the working below the boxes is the derivation. Margin remarks and ellipses are
deliberate. **They run ahead of the card** — check what is already on their page before posing a
mechanical check. Plain questions get `note`, never `wrong`. Pictures are instructions. Recurring
slip: writing gkg⁻¹ as gg⁻¹ — fix silently.
