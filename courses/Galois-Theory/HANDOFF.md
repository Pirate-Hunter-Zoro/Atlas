The run is all eleven exercises of chapter 4. Order: 4.1, 4.4, 4.6, 4.5,
then 4.2, 4.7-4.11, with 4.3 last. 4.1 is closed; 4.4 is half done.

Where they got to. 4.1 is finished and correct: tower law, the complete
degree-7 pair list (7,1) and (1,7), both degree-1 cases giving L = K and
M = L, and the final answer that the only intermediate fields are K and L.
None of that gets re-taught. On 4.4 they proved x^3 + 3x + 1 irreducible
over Q unaided and on the first attempt: constant term 1, so candidates 1
and -1, evaluated to 5 and -3, neither zero, no rational root, hence
irreducible.

What was wrong. Nothing this session. The only gap was a step they skipped
rather than missed: they wrote "hence irreducible" without saying why no
rational root forces it, which needs degree 3 splitting only as 1 + 2. Card
0007 supplies that line in passing instead of spending a turn on it.

The earlier real error, for context: on 4.1 they reached for a
contradiction where none was needed, and fed the spanning property an
arbitrary element instead of 1. Proof shape, never algebra. That is the
thing to watch.

Next thing to teach. Card 0007 is open and asks for alpha^{-1} alone, from
alpha^3 + 3alpha = -1 and factoring alpha out. Expect -alpha^2 - 3. Then
(1 + alpha)^{-1}, which is the actual content of 4.4: substituting
beta = 1 + alpha, or clearing denominators against the cubic. Do not let
them stop at alpha^{-1}; the easy half feels like the exercise and is not.

How this student works. Their arithmetic and their algebra are reliable;
the gaps are structural. They skip a warm-up they judge beneath the real
exercise, so do not re-pose a skipped one. They say plainly where they are
stuck. They leave the last justifying line unwritten when they think it is
obvious -- supply it, do not bill a turn for it.
