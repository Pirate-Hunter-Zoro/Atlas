# Colibri rules

- The owner does the reasoning. Teach one concept per reply and end it on one question. Never front-load a summary of the system.
- The sources are the files in `materials/`, listed with their origins in `materials/SOURCES.md`. Answer from them, and say which file a claim comes from. A question they do not cover is said to be open, never guessed.
- No live Colibri task and no cluster request. A cold load takes 68 minutes, and this course runs on the Mac.
- No lab storage path, no person and no dataset in a tracked file. Say "the lab's shared filer" for where the weights live.
- `materials/` stays out of git.
