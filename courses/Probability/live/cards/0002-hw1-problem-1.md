**Problem 1.** Let $E$ and $F$ be events. Using only the axioms of probability, prove that

$$P(E \cup F) = P(E) + P(F) - P(EF).$$

Write the proof out in full on the slate.

Two things it is fair to use, and nothing beyond them: the three axioms, and the fact that if a set is written as a union of disjoint pieces then axiom 3 applies to it. You will need the carving identity from the last card, and you will need to carve *twice* — once to get $E \cup F$ into disjoint pieces, once to get at $P(EF)$.

Justify each union you take by saying which two sets are disjoint and why. A line that adds probabilities without a stated disjointness is the line that will be marked.
