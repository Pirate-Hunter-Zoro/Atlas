The structure is correct and it is the whole difficulty of the problem: you
wanted $P(C \mid W)$, you did not have it, you had $P(W \mid C)$, so you turned
the conditioning around and rebuilt the denominator by splitting $W$ across the
three stores. Line 1 to line 3 is Bayes with the law of total probability under
it, and you produced it without being shown either. That part stands.

Two numbers are wrong, and both came off the problem sheet rather than out of
your reasoning.

**The total.** You have $215$ in every denominator. Add the three staff counts
again: $50 + 75 + 100$.

**The last percentage.** The three figures are $50$, $60$ and $70$ percent. Your
numerator uses $0.7$ for store $C$. Your denominator's third term uses $0.75$.
One of those two is not on the sheet, and they are supposed to be the same
number.

---

Fix both and finish the arithmetic. The answer is a clean fraction — if it comes
out ugly, one of the numbers is still wrong.
