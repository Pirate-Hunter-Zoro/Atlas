The identity is right, and it is the hint's argument in your own notation rather than a transcription of it.

The step that carries it is the last one. Everything before it is bookkeeping — mutually exclusive events over $n=1,2,\dots$, countable additivity, geometric sum, giving $P(E)/\bigl(1-P(E^cF^c)\bigr)$. Then:

$$1-P(E^cF^c) \;=\; 1-P\bigl((E\cup F)^c\bigr) \;=\; P(E\cup F) \;=\; P(E)+P(F),$$

De Morgan, complement, and only then mutual exclusivity. Your right-hand chain does exactly that and labels the last equality with the axiom it uses. Nothing in it is assumed.

**The aside.** Also correct, and it is the thing I said you were assuming during Boole. $\bigcup F_i \subseteq \bigcup E_i$ is immediate from $F_i \subseteq E_i$; the other inclusion is the one with content, and your induction gets it. The branch that used to trap you — $e \notin E_n$ — is now handled in one line: $e$ is in the union of the $n$ sets and not in the $n$th, so it is in an earlier one. That gap is closed. $\bigcup_{i=1}^n E_i = \bigcup_{i=1}^n F_i$ is now something you have proved rather than something you declared.
