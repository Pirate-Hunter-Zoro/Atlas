**Problem 2 — Ross ch. 1, exercise 8.**

If $P(E) = 0.9$ and $P(F) = 0.8$, show that

$$P(EF) \ \ge\ 0.7.$$

In general, show that

$$P(EF) \ \ge\ P(E) + P(F) - 1.$$

This is known as **Bonferroni's inequality**.
