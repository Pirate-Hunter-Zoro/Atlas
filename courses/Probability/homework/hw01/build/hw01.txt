Problem 12. Let E and F be mutually exclusive events in the sample space of an experiment.
Suppose that the experiment is repeated until either event E or event F occurs. What does the
sample space of this new super experiment look like? Show that the probability that event E occurs
before event F is P (E)/[P (E) + P (F )].
Hint: Argue that the probability that the original experiment is performed n times and E
appears on the nth time is P (E) × (1 − p)n−1 , n = 1, 2, . . ., where p = P (E) + P (F ). Add these
probabilities to get the desired answer.
